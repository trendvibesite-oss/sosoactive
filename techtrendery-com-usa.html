How Should You Evaluate Technology Articles?

Not every article on the internet should be treated as equally authoritative.

This is true for TechTrendery and every other general-interest publication.

Before relying on a technology article, consider five simple questions.

1. Who Wrote It?

Look for an identifiable author and consider whether the subject matches the author's apparent expertise.

2. When Was It Published?

Technology changes rapidly.

An article that was accurate two years ago may no longer describe current software, pricing, policies, or capabilities.

3. Does It Provide Evidence?

Important claims should ideally be supported by:

  • Official documentation
  • Research
  • Company announcements
  • Government sources
  • Industry data
  • Original reporting

4. Is the Information Specific?

An article that explains exactly how something works is usually more useful than one that relies entirely on phrases such as “revolutionary” or “game-changing.”

5. Can You Confirm Important Claims?

If something affects your business, finances, privacy, security, or purchasing decision, verify it independently.

This simple habit can prevent many bad decisions.
