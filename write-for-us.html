Editorial Guidelines & Submission Rules

To maintain high editorial standards for our readers, please adhere strictly to the following rules:

  1. Original Content Only: Content must be 100% unique, plagiarism-free, and unpublished on any other website.
  2. Word Count: Minimum 900+ words. In-depth, comprehensive articles (1,200+ words) receive priority publishing.
  3. Structure & Readability: Use clear subheadings (H2, H3), short bullet points, and clean paragraph breaks.
  4. Grammar & Accuracy: Ensure your draft is spell-checked, grammatically sound, and supported by accurate facts.
  5. Images: Include at least 1 high-resolution copyright-free featured image with proper attribution.
  6. Contextual Backlinks: Relevant, non-promotional editorial links are accepted. Spammy, affiliate, or broken links will be removed.
  7. Author Bio: Include a brief 2-3 sentence author biography along with your submission.

How to Submit Your Guest Post

Submitting your post is simple:

1. Prepare your article draft in Google Docs or Microsoft Word format.
2. Ensure all editorial guidelines above are followed.
3. Email your pitch or completed draft directly to plusplymarketing@gmail.com with the subject line "Guest Post Submission: [Your Proposed Article Title]".
