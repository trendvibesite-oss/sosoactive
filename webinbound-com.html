What Is Inbound Marketing?

The word “inbound” in WebInbound is particularly relevant to digital marketing.

Inbound marketing is a strategy focused on attracting potential customers through useful content, search visibility, educational resources, social media, and other channels rather than relying entirely on interruptive advertising.

A typical inbound marketing process looks like this:

Attract → Engage → Convert → Retain

For example:

  1. A user searches for a solution.
  2. They discover a useful article.
  3. They visit the website.
  4. They explore a product or service.
  5. They eventually become a lead or customer.

This approach is closely connected with SEO and content marketing.
