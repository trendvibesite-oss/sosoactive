# SosoActive Image Registry & Publishing Guidelines

## 🚨 MANDATORY RULE: Zero-Duplicate Image Policy
**NEVER reuse or copy an existing stock image from an old article when publishing any new article.**

For EVERY new blog article:
1. **100% Fresh & Unique**: Sourced with completely new, unique Unsplash photo IDs specifically curated for that article's niche.
2. **Quantity**: Exactly 1 Featured Hero Image + 2-3 Contextual Body Images.
3. **Format & Dimensions**: Aspect Ratio 16:9 (`800x450 px`), WebP format (`quality=82`), strictly `<40 KB` per file.
4. **Descriptive SEO Alt Tags**: Meaningful `alt="..."` and `<figcaption>` describing the exact graphic/topic.
5. **Root-Relative Paths**: Always use `/assets/images/<slug>-<topic>.webp` across both standalone `.html` and `<slug>/index.html`.
